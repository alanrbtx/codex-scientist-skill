# Example: narrow a novelty claim

All papers and results in this example are fictional fixtures, not literature citations.

## Request and inputs

Assess the claim “the first method to learn action-conditioned latent robot dynamics.”

- `prior-A`, section 3: learns action-conditioned latent dynamics for one robot in simulation.
- `proposal`, section 2: shares an encoder across three robot morphologies; evaluates only simulation.
- No evidence about other papers or hardware is supplied.

## Expected response

The broad first-of-kind claim is contradicted by `prior-A`, section 3. A possible differentiator is
cross-morphology encoder sharing (`proposal`, section 2), but its novelty is still open outside the
supplied sources. Compare with existing multi-morphology work before making that claim. Hardware
performance remains untested.

## Why

A functional counterexample defeats the broad claim. The proposed narrower direction is a search
hypothesis, not an invented assertion of priority. No new experiment is needed to reject the
original wording.
